# The World in One Day — Thursday 19 November 2026

A newspaper-front-page image summing up the world's news of
Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn with PIL by `draw.py` and shipped as
`entry.png`. `main.py` copies it to the grader's output directory.

## Stories covered

- **Lead — Gaming:** Grand Theft Auto VI launches worldwide after 13 years,
  rolling out at midnight local time on PlayStation 5 and Xbox; early
  reviews score 99 on Metacritic, pre-orders put at about 16 million copies.
- **Washington:** House Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker,
  251–11; if elected by the full House on 3 January he would be the first
  Black Speaker.
- **The Hague:** The ICJ delivers its judgment today on whether Myanmar
  committed genocide against the Rohingya.
- **Shenzhen:** Trump, Xi and Putin hold trilateral talks on the APEC
  sidelines, on nuclear arms cuts and the US war with Iran.
- **Weather:** Cyclone Akvan nears India's east coast, set to cross between
  Machilipatnam and Puri.
- **The Gulf:** Iran widens its attacks, targeting a US base in Kuwait and
  striking Bahrain again, after US jets hit a missile site on Qeshm Island.
- **Space:** Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth; NASA is to confirm
  the milestone today.
- **In brief:** EU proposes a €45bn Ukraine loan; Pew says Islam is on track
  to be the world's largest religion by 2035; Fed minutes point to further
  rate rises; Modi meets the Prince and Princess of Wales.

Every headline is a real event reported on 19 November 2026, verified
against news sources at build time. Events scheduled later in the day
(the ICJ judgment, the Shenzhen talks, the Voyager confirmation) are
worded as happening today, with no outcome claimed in advance.
